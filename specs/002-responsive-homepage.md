# Specification 002: Responsive Homepage & Brand Showcase
**Document ID:** SPEC-002  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** UI/UX Lead & Frontend Architect  

---

## 1. Business Context
The homepage is the primary entry point for enterprise buyers, prospective partners, and industry analysts. It must immediately communicate Nexora's core engineering offerings, establish technical credibility, highlight key case metrics, and guide users toward consultation enquiries.

## 2. User Stories
- As an enterprise CTO, I want to immediately understand Nexora's service domains so I can determine if they fit our digital transformation roadmap.
- As a prospective client, I want to see tangible case study outcomes and customer impact metrics so I can trust their delivery capabilities.

## 3. Functional Requirements
- **FR-2.1 Hero Section:** Value proposition headline, subheadline, primary CTA ("Schedule Engineering Consultation"), secondary CTA ("Explore Services").
- **FR-2.2 Capability Grid:** 6 core service cards linking to respective service detail pages.
- **FR-2.3 Industry Solutions:** Overview of solutions across FinTech, Healthcare, Smart Industrial, and Retail.
- **FR-2.4 Proof & Outcomes:** Factual delivery impact statistics (e.g., "50+ Enterprise Deployments", "99.99% Availability Delivered").
- **FR-2.5 Conversion CTA:** Inline contact trigger linking directly to `/contact`.

## 4. UI/UX & Responsive Requirements
- Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 across all typography.
- Breakpoints at 640px, 768px, 1024px, 1280px.
- Zero horizontal layout shift (CLS < 0.05).
