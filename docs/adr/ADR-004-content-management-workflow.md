# ADR-004: Content Lifecycle & Publishing Workflow
**Status:** Approved  
**Deciders:** Principal Architect, Product Manager  
**Date:** 2026-11-11  

---

## 1. Context and Problem Statement
To maintain high factual accuracy and brand reputation, content generated by human authors or AI content agents must not publish directly to the live website without review.

## 2. Decision Outcome
Chosen option: **Three-State State Machine (`draft` -> `under_review` -> `published` / `archived`)**.

### State Rules
1. `draft`: Content authored by an editor or generated by the Content Generation Agent. Invisible to public queries.
2. `under_review`: Content submitted for human review. Verifies factual claims, client attributions, and SEO metadata.
3. `published`: Approved by an administrator. Included in public searches, RSS, and `sitemap.xml`.
4. `archived`: Retired content retained for historical audit trails with 301 redirect or 410 Gone status.
